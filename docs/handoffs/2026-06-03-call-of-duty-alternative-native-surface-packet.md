## Objective

Identify a native `call_of_duty` HUD surface family that is:

- repeatable
- visible across many clips
- not dependent on rare events
- not dependent on editorial overlays
- suitable for runtime extraction

## Selection Criteria

A candidate family should satisfy most of:

### Visibility

Appears in many matches.

### Native HUD

Generated by the game itself.

### Repeatability

Occurs frequently enough to validate.

### Stability

Consistent placement and styling.

### Runtime Relevance

Correlates with meaningful gameplay events.
